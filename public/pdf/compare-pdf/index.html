Frequently asked questions

Does the tool compare the visual layout?

No. It compares selectable text only, not formatting, page layout, images or scanned appearance.

Can it compare scanned PDFs?

Not reliably. Image-only scans require OCR, which is not included in this tool.

Are both PDF files uploaded?

The supported text extraction and comparison are performed locally in your browser.
