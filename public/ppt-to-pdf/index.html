Frequently asked questions

How do I convert PPT to PDF?

Choose a .pptx presentation in the PowerPoint to PDF workspace, run the converter and download the generated PDF.

Can I convert PPTX to PDF for free?

The current converter is available in the browser without an account.

Does it support old PPT files?

No. The current tool accepts the modern .pptx format, not legacy .ppt files.

Will the PDF look exactly like my PowerPoint?

Not necessarily. Image-based slides created by AP Tools are preserved best. Other presentations use best-effort text extraction and may lose layout, charts, animations, fonts or objects.

Are my presentation files uploaded?

The supported conversion runs locally in your browser; files are not uploaded to an AP Creative Tools processing server.
